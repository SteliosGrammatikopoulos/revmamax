# RevmaMax Landing Page - Design Brainstorm

## Context
Μετατροπή του Facebook ad σε πλήρες landing page για υπηρεσία ηλεκτρικού ρεύματος.
Brand colors: Navy Blue (#0D1B3E, #1A3A6B), Bright Blue (#2563EB), Yellow (#FBBF24)

---

<response>
<idea>
**Design Movement**: Dark Energy / Neo-Industrial
**Core Principles**:
1. Dark navy backgrounds with electric energy effects (lightning, glow)
2. High contrast yellow/white text on dark backgrounds
3. Bold, impactful typography with strong hierarchy
4. Conversion-focused layout with clear CTA

**Color Philosophy**: Deep navy (#0D1B3E to #1A3A6B gradient) as base — evokes trust, professionalism, energy. Yellow (#FBBF24) as accent for CTAs and highlights — evokes electricity, urgency, warmth. Bright blue (#3B82F6) for secondary elements.

**Layout Paradigm**: Full-bleed hero with centered content, asymmetric benefit cards, sticky CTA bar

**Signature Elements**:
1. Lightning bolt SVG animations in background
2. Glowing logo with energy halo effect
3. Yellow CTA button with hover pulse animation

**Interaction Philosophy**: Smooth scroll reveals, hover glow effects, button pulse animations

**Animation**: Entrance animations on scroll, lightning flicker effect on hero, CTA button pulse

**Typography System**: Montserrat Bold for headlines (power/impact), Inter for body (readability)
</idea>
